## Exercise 16.1: Complete the sentences with the past perfect continuous.
*(Chia động từ trong ngoặc ở thì Quá khứ hoàn thành tiếp diễn — `had been + V-ing`)*

1. He (wait) [had been waiting] for two hours when the bus finally came.
2. The company (lose) [had been losing] money for years before it was restructured.
3. The team (work) [had been working] on the campaign for months before the launch.
4. By the time the shipment arrived, the store (wait) [had been waiting] for three weeks.
5. He (call) [had been calling] the client all morning before he finally got through.
6. The staff were exhausted because they (work) [had been working] overtime all week.

> [!success]- 🔑 Đáp án & Giải thích 16.1
> 1. **had been waiting** (`for two hours` + sự việc chặn lại ở quá khứ `the bus came` $\rightarrow$ nhấn mạnh quãng thời gian chờ; `wait $\rightarrow$ waiting`).
> 2. **had been losing** (`for years before it was restructured` $\rightarrow$ hành động kéo dài trước mốc quá khứ; `lose $\rightarrow$ losing`).
> 3. **had been working** (`for months before the launch` $\rightarrow$ quá trình chuẩn bị kéo dài; chủ ngữ `the team` + `had` không đổi).
> 4. **had been waiting** (`By the time` + Quá khứ đơn `arrived`; vế còn lại nhấn mạnh thời gian chờ 3 tuần).
> 5. **had been calling** (`all morning before he finally got through` $\rightarrow$ hành động lặp lại nhiều lần trước mốc quá khứ; `call $\rightarrow$ calling`).
> 6. **had been working** (Vế sau `were exhausted` là dấu hiệu ở quá khứ $\rightarrow$ suy ra nguyên nhân: họ **đã làm thêm giờ** suốt tuần).

## Exercise 16.2: Past perfect continuous or past perfect simple?
*(Chọn `had been + V-ing` hoặc `had + V3` cho động từ trong ngoặc)*

1. She (write) [had been writing] the report, so the document was still open on her screen.
2. She (write) [had written] the report, so she emailed it to the client immediately.
3. I (know) [had known] her for years, so I could recommend her confidently.
4. The auditors (check) [had been checking] the accounts all afternoon before they found the error.
5. By 6 P.M. the technicians (repair) [had repaired] the printer, and the staff could use it again.
6. The sales team (prepare) [had been preparing] the presentation for weeks before the meeting.

> [!success]- 🔑 Đáp án & Giải thích 16.2
> 1. **had been writing** (Vế sau `the document was still open` $\rightarrow$ báo cáo **chưa xong**, nhấn mạnh hành động $\rightarrow$ tiếp diễn).
> 2. **had written** (Vế sau `she emailed it immediately` $\rightarrow$ **đã viết xong**, nhấn mạnh kết quả $\rightarrow$ đơn).
> 3. **had known** — **bẫy stative verb**: `know` là động từ trạng thái nên **không chia tiếp diễn**, dù có `for years`. Loại ~~had been knowing~~.
> 4. **had been checking** (`all afternoon` + `before they found the error` $\rightarrow$ hoạt động kiểm tra kéo dài, nhấn mạnh quá trình).
> 5. **had repaired** (`By 6 P.M.` + kết quả rõ `the staff could use it again` $\rightarrow$ việc sửa đã **hoàn tất**).
> 6. **had been preparing** (`for weeks before the meeting` $\rightarrow$ quãng thời gian chuẩn bị kéo dài, nhấn mạnh quá trình).

## Exercise 16.3: Complete with before / by the time and the past perfect continuous.
*(Hoàn thành câu, dùng `before` hoặc `by the time` + thì Quá khứ hoàn thành tiếp diễn cho hành động kéo dài)*

1. The staff (use) [had been using] the old software for a decade by the time the new system was installed.
2. He (try) [had been trying] to reach the supplier all morning before he finally got an answer.
3. The company (lose) [had been losing] customers for two years before it changed its pricing policy.
4. By the time the manager arrived, the team (discuss) [had been discussing] the budget for over an hour.
5. She (work) [had been working] at the branch for five years before she was promoted to head office.

> [!success]- 🔑 Đáp án & Giải thích 16.3
> 1. **had been using** (`for a decade` + `by the time the new system was installed` $\rightarrow$ hành động kéo dài trước mốc quá khứ; `use $\rightarrow$ using`).
> 2. **had been trying** (`all morning` + hành động lặp lại `before he finally got an answer` $\rightarrow$ tiếp diễn).
> 3. **had been losing** (`for two years before it changed its pricing policy` $\rightarrow$ quá trình kéo dài, `lose $\rightarrow$ losing`).
> 4. **had been discussing** (`for over an hour` + `By the time the manager arrived` $\rightarrow$ cuộc thảo luận đã diễn ra một tiếng đồng hồ; `discuss $\rightarrow$ discussing`).
> 5. **had been working** (`for five years before she was promoted` $\rightarrow$ nhấn mạnh quãng thời gian làm việc; chủ ngữ `she` vẫn dùng `had`).

## Exercise 16.4: TOEIC Part 5 Mini-drill — chọn đáp án đúng nhất.

1. The team \_\_\_\_\_\_ on the campaign for months before the launch.  
   (A) works · (B) has been working · (C) had been working · (D) worked  
   $\rightarrow$ `C` *(Ví dụ)*
2. By the time the shipment arrived, the store \_\_\_\_\_\_ for three weeks.  
   (A) had been waiting · (B) has been waiting · (C) waits · (D) was waiting  
   $\rightarrow$ [A]
3. I \_\_\_\_\_\_ Mr. Park for years, so I had no doubt about his ability.  
   (A) had been knowing · (B) had known · (C) have been knowing · (D) was knowing  
   $\rightarrow$ [B]
4. The accountants \_\_\_\_\_\_ the figures all afternoon before they discovered the discrepancy.  
   (A) had been checking · (B) had checked · (C) have checked · (D) check  
   $\rightarrow$ [A]
5. Ms. Reed \_\_\_\_\_\_ the branch for five years before she was promoted.  
   (A) had been managing · (B) manages · (C) has managed · (D) is managing  
   $\rightarrow$ [A]

> [!success]- 🔑 Đáp án & Giải thích 16.4
> 1. **(C) had been working** — `for months before the launch` $\rightarrow$ hành động kéo dài trước một mốc quá khứ; loại (B) ~~has been working~~ vì câu có mốc quá khứ `before the launch`.
> 2. **(A) had been waiting** — `By the time the shipment arrived` + `for three weeks` $\rightarrow$ `had been + V-ing`; loại (B) vì không dùng Hiện tại hoàn thành tiếp diễn trong bối cảnh quá khứ.
> 3. **(B) had known** — **bẫy stative verb**: `know` **không chia tiếp diễn**, dù có `for years`. Loại (A) ~~had been knowing~~.
> 4. **(A) had been checking** — `all afternoon before they discovered` $\rightarrow$ nhấn mạnh quá trình kiểm tra kéo dài. Loại (B) vì `had checked` ngụ ý việc kiểm tra đã xong trước đó.
> 5. **(A) had been managing** — `for five years before she was promoted` $\rightarrow$ quãng thời gian kéo dài trước mốc quá khứ; `had` không đổi theo chủ ngữ.
