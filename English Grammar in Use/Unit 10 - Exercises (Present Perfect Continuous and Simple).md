## Exercise 10.1: Chọn dạng đúng — `have been doing` hay `have done`?
*(Điền dạng đúng của động từ trong ngoặc)*

1. A: Is the meeting room ready? — B: Yes, we (prepare) [have prepared] everything, so we can start now.
2. A: Your eyes are red. — B: I (stare) [have been staring] at the screen all morning.
3. The report is finished. I (write) [have written] the conclusion.
4. The paint on the wall is still wet. We (paint) [have been painting] the lobby.
5. Sales (grow) [have been growing] steadily over the past two quarters.
6. We (receive) [have received] twelve applications so far.

> [!success]- 🔑 Đáp án & Giải thích 10.1
> 1. **have prepared** — vế sau `so we can start now` = **kết quả đã hoàn tất** $\rightarrow$ Hiện tại hoàn thành **đơn**.
> 2. **have been staring** — `all morning` + dấu hiệu mắt đỏ = **quá trình kéo dài** $\rightarrow$ Hiện tại hoàn thành **tiếp diễn**.
> 3. **have written** — `The report is finished` = **đã xong** $\rightarrow$ dạng **đơn**.
> 4. **have been painting** — `The paint is still wet` = **chưa xong** $\rightarrow$ dạng **tiếp diễn**.
> 5. **have been growing** — `over the past two quarters` + xu hướng đang thay đổi $\rightarrow$ dạng **tiếp diễn**.
> 6. **have received** — `so far` + **số lượng cụ thể** (twelve applications) $\rightarrow$ dạng **đơn**.

## Exercise 10.2: Đúng hay sai? Sửa lại câu sai.
*(Câu nào đúng ghi `OK`, câu nào sai thì viết lại cho đúng)*

1. I've been knowing Mr. Diaz since 2015.  
   $\rightarrow$ [I've known Mr. Diaz since 2015.]
2. She's been working here for three years.  
   $\rightarrow$ [OK — Đúng]
3. How long have you been having this laptop?  
   $\rightarrow$ [How long have you had this laptop?]
4. The manager has been approving the budget, so we can proceed.  
   $\rightarrow$ [The manager has approved the budget, so we can proceed.]
5. They've been discussing the contract all afternoon.  
   $\rightarrow$ [OK — Đúng]
6. I've been finishing the quarterly report, so I can send it now.  
   $\rightarrow$ [I've finished the quarterly report, so I can send it now.]

> [!success]- 🔑 Đáp án & Giải thích 10.2
> 1. **Sai** $\rightarrow$ **I've known Mr. Diaz since 2015.** (`know` là stative verb, **không chia tiếp diễn** dù có `since`).
> 2. **Đúng** — `work` là hành động, `for three years` nhấn mạnh quá trình vẫn đang tiếp diễn; có thể đổi thành *She's worked here for three years* mà nghĩa gần như tương đương.
> 3. **Sai** $\rightarrow$ **How long have you had this laptop?** (`have` = **sở hữu** $\rightarrow$ dùng dạng đơn).
> 4. **Sai** $\rightarrow$ **The manager has approved the budget, so we can proceed.** (vế sau `so we can proceed` = kết quả đã xong $\rightarrow$ dạng đơn).
> 5. **Đúng** — `all afternoon` + hành động còn kéo dài $\rightarrow$ tiếp diễn.
> 6. **Sai** $\rightarrow$ **I've finished the quarterly report, so I can send it now.** (`so I can send it now` = **đã xong**; `have been finishing` ngụ ý vẫn chưa làm xong).

## Exercise 10.3: TOEIC Part 5 Mini-drill — chọn đáp án đúng nhất.

1. The research team \_\_\_\_\_\_ the survey results, so the report can be submitted today.  
   (A) has been analyzing · (B) has analyzed · (C) is analyzing · (D) analyzes  
   $\rightarrow$ `B` *(Ví dụ)*
2. Ms. Okafor \_\_\_\_\_\_ for this company since she graduated from college.  
   (A) works · (B) worked · (C) has been working · (D) is working  
   $\rightarrow$ [C]
3. I \_\_\_\_\_\_ the new marketing manager since the orientation session.  
   (A) have been knowing · (B) know · (C) have known · (D) am knowing  
   $\rightarrow$ [C]
4. Electricity costs \_\_\_\_\_\_ significantly over the past six months.  
   (A) have been climbing · (B) climb · (C) climbed · (D) are climbing  
   $\rightarrow$ [A]
5. Sorry for the delay — we \_\_\_\_\_\_ your order, and it will be shipped tomorrow.  
   (A) have been processing · (B) have processed · (C) process · (D) processed  
   $\rightarrow$ [B]

> [!success]- 🔑 Đáp án & Giải thích 10.3
> 1. **(B) has analyzed** — `so the report can be submitted today` = **kết quả đã hoàn tất**.
> 2. **(C) has been working** — `since she graduated` + hành động còn tiếp diễn; `Ms. Okafor` số ít $\rightarrow$ `has been working`.
> 3. **(C) have known** — **bẫy stative verb**: `know` không chia tiếp diễn; loại (A) ~~have been knowing~~, loại (D) ~~am knowing~~.
> 4. **(A) have been climbing** — `over the past six months` + xu hướng đang tăng $\rightarrow$ Hiện tại hoàn thành **tiếp diễn**; `costs` số nhiều $\rightarrow$ `have been climbing`.
> 5. **(B) have processed** — vế sau `it will be shipped tomorrow` cho thấy việc xử lý **đã xong**; `have been processing` sẽ ngụ ý *vẫn đang xử lý, chưa xong*.

## Exercise 10.4: Viết 4 câu về công việc học TOEIC của bạn.
*(Dùng cả hai dạng: 2 câu `I've been + V-ing` cho quá trình, 2 câu `I've + V3` cho kết quả)*

> **Gợi ý:** `study English`, `review grammar units`, `learn new words`, `take practice tests`, `watch English videos`, `finish exercises`

1. `I've been studying English for six months.` *(Ví dụ — quá trình)*
2. [I've been reviewing grammar units every day.]
3. [I've been learning new words with flashcards.]
4. [I've finished eight grammar units this month.]
5. [I've taken three practice tests so far.]

> [!success]- 🔑 Đáp án & Giải thích 10.4 (gợi ý)
> - **Quá trình (have been + V-ing):** *I've been studying English for six months.* / *I've been reviewing grammar units every day.* / *I've been learning new words with flashcards.*
> - **Kết quả (have + V3):** *I've finished eight grammar units this month.* / *I've taken three practice tests so far.* / *I've learned 120 new words so far.*
> - Tự kiểm tra: câu **quá trình** thường đi với `every day / for + thời gian / all week`; câu **kết quả** thường đi với `so far / this month / số lượng cụ thể`.
