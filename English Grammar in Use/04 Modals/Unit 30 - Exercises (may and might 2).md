## Exercise 30.1: Complete with `might` or `might have` and the verb.

1. If the price were lower, we [] a larger order. (place)
2. If the system had been tested, the error [] earlier. (detect)
3. With more advertising, sales [] faster. (grow)
4. Without your reminder, I [] the deadline. (miss)
5. If the road is closed, the courier [] late. (arrive)

## Exercise 30.2: Complete with `may/might as well` or permission `may`.

1. The meeting is cancelled, so we [] to the office. (return)
2. The report is ready; we [] it now. (submit)
3. [] I leave these documents at reception?
4. Employees [] the training room after 3 p.m. (use)
5. There is no earlier flight, so we [] the evening one. (take)

> 2. **may/might as well submit** — chọn đúng cấu trúc và mốc thời gian của câu.
> 3. **May** — chọn đúng cấu trúc và mốc thời gian của câu.
> 4. **may use** — chọn đúng cấu trúc và mốc thời gian của câu.
> 5. **might as well take** — chọn đúng cấu trúc và mốc thời gian của câu.

## Exercise 30.4: TOEIC Part 5 Mini-drill

1. If demand increased, the factory _____ hire additional staff.  
   (A) might · (B) might have · (C) may as · (D) might to
2. Without the technician’s help, we _____ lost the customer data.  
   (A) might · (B) might have · (C) may well to · (D) might had
3. The last train has left, so we _____ take a taxi.  
   (A) might as well · (B) might have · (C) may to · (D) might as well to
4. _____ I submit the application electronically?  
   (A) Might have · (B) May · (C) May as well · (D) Am able
5. If the invoice had arrived earlier, payment _____ processed yesterday.  
   (A) might be · (B) might have been · (C) may have process · (D) might to be

> [!success]- 🔑 Đáp án & Giải thích 30.4
> 1. **(A)** — Kết quả giả định hiện tại/tương lai: `might + V`.
> 2. **(B)** — Kết quả giả định quá khứ: `might have + V3`.
> 3. **(A)** — Không còn lựa chọn tốt hơn: `might as well + V`.
> 4. **(B)** — Xin phép trang trọng dùng `May I + V`.
> 5. **(B)** — Bị động giả định quá khứ: `might have been + V3`.
