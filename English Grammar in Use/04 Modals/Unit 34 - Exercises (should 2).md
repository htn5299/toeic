## Exercise 34.1: Complete with `should` and the correct verb form.

1. The package [should arrive] by Friday. (arrive)
2. The update [should improve] processing speed. (improve)
3. There [should be] enough seats for everyone. (be)
4. The repair [should not take] more than two hours. (not take)
5. Sales [should increase] after the promotion begins. (increase)

> [!success]- 🔑 Đáp án & Giải thích 34.1
> 1. **should arrive** — kỳ vọng tương lai dựa trên lịch trình: `should + V`.
> 2. **should improve** — dự đoán hợp lý từ thông số kỹ thuật.
> 3. **should be** — kỳ vọng về số lượng chỗ ngồi.
> 4. **should not take** — phủ định kỳ vọng: `should not + V`.
> 5. **should increase** — dự báo doanh số sau chiến dịch quảng cáo.

## Exercise 34.2: Rewrite using `that + should` or `if/Should … should`.

1. We recommend: each applicant submits two references.  
   → **We recommend that each applicant should submit two references.**
2. The client insisted: correct the error immediately.  
   → **The client insisted that the error should be corrected immediately.**
3. If you need assistance, contact reception. (formal)  
   → **If you should need assistance, contact reception.**
4. If you have questions, email HR. (inversion)  
   → **Should you have any questions, email HR.**
5. The manager suggested: postpone the launch.  
   → **The manager suggested that the launch should be postponed.**

> [!success]- 🔑 Đáp án & Giải thích 34.2
> 1. **that … should submit** — sau `recommend that` dùng `should + V` (hoặc bare infinitive).
> 2. **that … should be corrected** — bị động: `should be + V3`; chủ ngữ là `the error`.
> 3. **If you should need** — thêm `should` vào mệnh đề `if` để trang trọng hơn.
> 4. **Should you have** — đảo ngữ điều kiện trang trọng: `Should + S + V`, bỏ `If`.
> 5. **that … should be postponed** — bị động: `should be + V3`; chủ ngữ là `the launch`.

## Exercise 34.3: Correct the mistake in each sentence.

1. The shipment should ~~arrives~~ **arrive** tomorrow.
2. We recommend that she should ~~submits~~ **submit** the form.
3. The client insisted that we should ~~corrected~~ **correct** the error.
4. If you should ~~to~~ need help, call reception.
5. Should you ~~will~~ have questions, contact HR.

> [!success]- 🔑 Đáp án & Giải thích 34.3
> 1. **should arrive** — sau modal (`should`) luôn dùng V nguyên thể, không chia.
> 2. **should submit** — sau `should` trong mệnh đề `that` dùng V nguyên thể.
> 3. **should correct** — sau `should` trong mệnh đề `that` dùng V nguyên thể.
> 4. **If you should need** — `should` không dùng kèm `to` trong cấu trúc điều kiện này.
> 5. **Should you have** — đảo ngữ điều kiện: không dùng `will` kèm `Should`.

## Exercise 34.4: TOEIC Part 5 Mini-drill

1. According to the schedule, the shipment _____ arrive before noon.  
   **(A) should** · (B) should to · (C) should have · (D) should be
2. The committee recommended that the proposal _____ revised.  
   **(A) should be** · (B) should is · (C) should to be · (D) has should
3. If you _____ require additional copies, contact the front desk.  
   **(A) should** · (B) should to · (C) will should · (D) have should
4. _____ you have any questions, please contact Human Resources.  
   **(A) Should** · (B) Would have · (C) Must · (D) Could have
5. The new routing system _____ reduce average delivery times.  
   **(A) should** · (B) should to · (C) should have been · (D) is should

> [!success]- 🔑 Đáp án & Giải thích 34.4
> 1. **(A) should** — kỳ vọng tương lai theo lịch: `should + V`; `should to` sai; `should have` chỉ dùng quá khứ.
> 2. **(A) should be** — bị động sau `recommend that`: `should be + V3 (revised)`.
> 3. **(A) should** — điều kiện trang trọng: `if + S + should + V`; không dùng `to` sau `should`.
> 4. **(A) Should** — đảo ngữ điều kiện trang trọng: `Should + S + V …`.
> 5. **(A) should** — kỳ vọng hợp lý dựa trên tính năng hệ thống: `should + V`.
