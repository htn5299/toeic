## Exercise 27.1: Complete with `could + V` or `could have + V3`.

1. We [] the workshop online next Friday. (hold)
2. The company [] money by booking earlier, but it waited. (save)
3. This software error [] tomorrow’s payroll run. (affect)
4. You [] the client before cancelling the order. (call)
5. The missing attachment [] the misunderstanding. (cause)

> [!success]- 🔑 Đáp án & Giải thích 27.1
> 1. **could hold** — chọn đúng cấu trúc và mốc thời gian của câu.
> 2. **could have saved** — chọn đúng cấu trúc và mốc thời gian của câu.
> 3. **could affect** — chọn đúng cấu trúc và mốc thời gian của câu.
> 4. **could have called** — chọn đúng cấu trúc và mốc thời gian của câu.
> 5. **could have caused** — chọn đúng cấu trúc và mốc thời gian của câu.

## Exercise 27.4: TOEIC Part 5 Mini-drill

1. The scheduling error _____ caused the duplicate appointment.  
   (A) could have · (B) could · (C) can have · (D) was could
2. We _____ postpone the product launch until next month.  
   (A) could have · (B) could · (C) could had · (D) have could
3. The assistant _____ authorized the transfer because she lacks permission.  
   (A) could not · (B) couldn’t have · (C) could have not · (D) can’t have to
4. You _____ informed accounting before changing the order.  
   (A) could · (B) could have · (C) could had · (D) were able
5. A lower price _____ attract more customers next quarter.  
   (A) could have · (B) could · (C) has could · (D) could to

> [!success]- 🔑 Đáp án & Giải thích 27.4
> 1. **(A)** — Khả năng về nguyên nhân quá khứ: `could have + V3`.
> 2. **(B)** — Đề xuất cho tương lai dùng `could + V`.
> 3. **(B)** — Suy luận bất khả thi trong quá khứ: `couldn’t have + V3`.
> 4. **(B)** — Phê bình việc quá khứ đã không làm.
> 5. **(B)** — Khả năng tương lai dùng `could + V`.
