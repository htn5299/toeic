## Exercise 15.1: Complete the sentences with the past perfect.
*(Chia động từ trong ngoặc ở thì Quá khứ hoàn thành — `had + V3`)*

1. When I arrived at the office, the meeting (already / start) [had already started].
2. She told me that she (see) [had seen] the report on the manager's desk.
3. By the time the auditors arrived, the accountants (prepare) [had prepared] all the documents.
4. The client called to say that he (not / receive) [hadn't received] the invoice.
5. After the manager (review) [had reviewed] the proposal, she approved it.
6. I couldn't find the conference room because I (never / be) [had never been] on that floor before.

> [!success]- 🔑 Đáp án & Giải thích 15.1
> 1. **had already started** (Cuộc họp bắt đầu **trước** khi tôi đến $\rightarrow$ việc sớm hơn chia `had + V3`; `already` đứng giữa `had` và V3).
> 2. **had seen** (Câu gián tiếp: lời nói trực tiếp *"I saw the report"* $\rightarrow$ lùi thì thành `had seen`).
> 3. **had prepared** (`By the time + mệnh đề quá khứ` $\rightarrow$ vế còn lại dùng Quá khứ hoàn thành; *prepare $\rightarrow$ prepared*).
> 4. **hadn't received** / **had not received** (Phủ định: `had not + V3`; *receive $\rightarrow$ received*).
> 5. **had reviewed** (`After` + hành động xảy ra trước $\rightarrow$ `had reviewed`; vế sau giữ Quá khứ đơn `approved`).
> 6. **had never been** (Trạng thái/trải nghiệm **trước** mốc quá khứ; `never` đứng giữa `had` và `been`).

## Exercise 15.2: Past simple or past perfect?
*(Chọn thì Quá khứ đơn hoặc Quá khứ hoàn thành cho động từ trong ngoặc)*

1. When I got to the station, the train (leave) [had left], so I missed it.
2. When I got to the station, the train (leave) [left] and I watched it go.
3. The assistant (tell) [told] me that the director (go) [had gone] to the branch office.
4. We (finish) [had finished] the inventory before the store (open) [opened].
5. She (open) [opened] the door and (walk) [walked] into the meeting room.
6. Until then, the company (never / face) [had never faced] such strong competition.

> [!success]- 🔑 Đáp án & Giải thích 15.2
> 1. **had left** (Tàu chạy **trước** khi tôi tới $\rightarrow$ tôi bị trễ; quay ngược về việc sớm hơn).
> 2. **left** (Tôi tới nơi **rồi mới thấy** tàu chạy $\rightarrow$ hai việc gần như đồng thời, kể tuần tự nên dùng Quá khứ đơn).
> 3. **told … had gone** (Động từ tường thuật `told` chia Quá khứ đơn; việc giám đốc đi **trước** khi được kể $\rightarrow$ `had gone`).
> 4. **had finished … opened** (Việc kiểm kê xong **trước** khi cửa hàng mở $\rightarrow$ `had finished`; `before` + mệnh đề Quá khứ đơn).
> 5. **opened … walked** (Chuỗi hai hành động kể theo đúng thứ tự, nối bằng `and` $\rightarrow$ **không** dùng `had`).
> 6. **had never faced** (`Until then` = tính đến mốc quá khứ đó $\rightarrow$ việc chưa từng xảy ra trước đó; `never` đứng giữa `had` và V3).

## Exercise 15.3: Join the sentences with after / before / by the time.
*(Nối hai câu, dùng `after`, `before` hoặc `by the time` kết hợp thì Quá khứ hoàn thành cho việc xảy ra trước)*

1. First the board approved the budget. Then the project started. (after)  
   $\rightarrow$ [After the board had approved the budget, the project started.]
2. First the courier delivered the samples. Then we held the meeting. (before)  
   $\rightarrow$ [The courier had delivered the samples before we held the meeting.]
3. I arrived at the conference. The keynote speaker had already begun. (by the time)  
   $\rightarrow$ [By the time I arrived at the conference, the keynote speaker had already begun.]
4. Mr. Kim checked the figures twice. Then he submitted the report. (after)  
   $\rightarrow$ [After Mr. Kim had checked the figures twice, he submitted the report.]
5. The client did not reply to the first email. That is why we sent a follow-up. (because)  
   $\rightarrow$ [Because the client hadn't replied to the first email, we sent a follow-up.]

> [!success]- 🔑 Đáp án & Giải thích 15.3
> 1. **After the board had approved the budget, the project started.** (`after` + việc xảy ra trước $\rightarrow$ `had approved`; vế sau Quá khứ đơn `started`).
> 2. **The courier had delivered the samples before we held the meeting.** (Việc giao mẫu xảy ra **trước** $\rightarrow$ `had delivered`; `before` + Quá khứ đơn).
> 3. **By the time I arrived at the conference, the keynote speaker had already begun.** (`By the time` + Quá khứ đơn `arrived`; việc đã bắt đầu trước đó $\rightarrow$ `had already begun`).
> 4. **After Mr. Kim had checked the figures twice, he submitted the report.** (Kiểm tra số liệu **trước** khi nộp $\rightarrow$ `had checked`; *check $\rightarrow$ checked*).
> 5. **Because the client hadn't replied to the first email, we sent a follow-up.** (Phủ định `hadn't + V3` cho việc chưa xảy ra trước mốc quá khứ; vế kết quả dùng Quá khứ đơn).

## Exercise 15.4: TOEIC Part 5 Mini-drill — chọn đáp án đúng nhất.

1. By the time the auditors \_\_\_\_\_\_, the accounting team had prepared all the documents.  
   (A) arrive · (B) arrived · (C) had arrived · (D) have arrived  
   $\rightarrow$ `B` *(Ví dụ)*
2. After the manager \_\_\_\_\_\_ the proposal, she approved it without any changes.  
   (A) reviews · (B) has reviewed · (C) had reviewed · (D) is reviewing  
   $\rightarrow$ [C]
3. Mr. Cole said that he \_\_\_\_\_\_ the expense claim the previous day.  
   (A) submits · (B) had submitted · (C) has submitted · (D) was submitting  
   $\rightarrow$ [B]
4. The technicians \_\_\_\_\_\_ the server before the morning shift began.  
   (A) had repaired · (B) have repaired · (C) repair · (D) are repairing  
   $\rightarrow$ [A]
5. It was the first time the company \_\_\_\_\_\_ at an international trade show.  
   (A) exhibits · (B) has exhibited · (C) had exhibited · (D) is exhibiting  
   $\rightarrow$ [C]

> [!success]- 🔑 Đáp án & Giải thích 15.4
> 1. **(B) arrived** — `By the time` + **Quá khứ đơn** cho mốc neo; vế còn lại đã có `had prepared`. Loại (C) vì không dùng `had` cho mốc neo.
> 2. **(C) had reviewed** — Kiểm tra đề xuất xảy ra **trước** khi phê duyệt $\rightarrow$ `had + V3`; vế sau `approved` giữ Quá khứ đơn.
> 3. **(B) had submitted** — Câu gián tiếp (`said that`) + `the previous day` $\rightarrow$ lùi thì về Quá khứ hoàn thành. Loại (C) ~~has submitted~~ vì không dùng Hiện tại hoàn thành trong mệnh đề tường thuật quá khứ.
> 4. **(A) had repaired** — Sửa server xong **trước** khi ca sáng bắt đầu (`before` + Quá khứ đơn `began`).
> 5. **(C) had exhibited** — Mẫu cố định `It was the first time + S + had + V3` (lần đầu tiên tính đến mốc quá khứ đó).
