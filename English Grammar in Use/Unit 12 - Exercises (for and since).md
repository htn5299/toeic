## Exercise 12.1: Điền `for` hoặc `since`.

1. We've been partners [for] over a decade.
2. The factory has been running [since] 5 o'clock this morning.
3. I haven't taken a day off [for] six months.
4. Sales have increased steadily [since] the new manager arrived.
5. Ms. Chen has been with the firm [for] twelve years.
6. Nothing has changed [since] our last meeting.

> [!success]- 🔑 Đáp án & Giải thích 12.1
> 1. **for** + khoảng thời gian (*over a decade*).
> 2. **since** + mốc thời gian (*5 o'clock this morning*).
> 3. **for** (*six months*).
> 4. **since** + mệnh đề (*the new manager arrived*).
> 5. **for** (*twelve years*).
> 6. **since** + cụm mốc thời gian (*our last meeting*).

## Exercise 12.2: Viết câu hỏi — `When … ?` hay `How long … ?`

1. Bạn muốn biết thời điểm Tom bắt đầu làm việc ở đây.  
   $\rightarrow$ `When did Tom start working here?` *(Ví dụ)*
2. Bạn muốn biết Tom đã làm việc ở đây bao lâu.  
   $\rightarrow$ [How long has Tom worked here?]
3. Bạn muốn biết thời điểm công ty được thành lập.  
   $\rightarrow$ [When was the company founded?]
4. Bạn muốn biết khách hàng đã chờ bao lâu.  
   $\rightarrow$ [How long has the customer been waiting?]
5. Bạn muốn biết giá bắt đầu tăng từ lúc nào.  
   $\rightarrow$ [Since when have prices been rising?]

> [!success]- 🔑 Đáp án & Giải thích 12.2
> 2. **How long has Tom worked here?** (hoặc *has Tom been working here?*) — hỏi khoảng thời gian $\rightarrow$ Present Perfect.
> 3. **When was the company founded?** — hỏi mốc thời gian $\rightarrow$ Past Simple + bị động.
> 4. **How long has the customer been waiting?** — hành động kéo dài $\rightarrow$ tiếp diễn.
> 5. **Since when have prices been rising?** — hỏi mốc bắt đầu của một xu hướng đang tiếp diễn.

## Exercise 12.3: Chọn thì đúng (Present Perfect hay Past Simple).

1. I (work) [have worked] here for five years and I'm still here.
2. I (work) [worked] there for five years, then I moved to another company.
3. She (live) [has lived] in Singapore since 2018.
4. He (live) [lived] in Bangkok for three years before he came back.
5. We (know) [have known] each other for a long time.
6. We (meet) [met] at a conference in 2022.

> [!success]- 🔑 Đáp án & Giải thích 12.3
> 1. **have worked** — `for five years` + vẫn đang làm $\rightarrow$ Present Perfect.
> 2. **worked** — có kết thúc rõ (`then I moved`) $\rightarrow$ Past Simple.
> 3. **has lived** — `since 2018` + còn tiếp diễn.
> 4. **lived** — giai đoạn đã kết thúc (`before he came back`).
> 5. **have known** — `know` là stative verb + `for a long time`.
> 6. **met** — mốc thời gian đã kết thúc (`in 2022`) $\rightarrow$ Past Simple.

## Exercise 12.4: TOEIC Part 5 Mini-drill.

1. Mr. Novak has been the regional director \_\_\_\_\_\_ 2019.  
   (A) for · (B) since · (C) during · (D) from  
   $\rightarrow$ `B` *(Ví dụ)*
2. The company has expanded rapidly \_\_\_\_\_\_ the past three years.  
   (A) since · (B) over · (C) from · (D) by  
   $\rightarrow$ [B]
3. \_\_\_\_\_\_ did the marketing team start the new campaign?  
   (A) How long · (B) When · (C) How often · (D) Since when  
   $\rightarrow$ [B]
4. The position has been vacant \_\_\_\_\_\_ three months.  
   (A) since · (B) for · (C) during · (D) within  
   $\rightarrow$ [B]
5. Nothing has been changed \_\_\_\_\_\_ the committee approved the original plan.  
   (A) for · (B) during · (C) since · (D) until  
   $\rightarrow$ [C]

> [!success]- 🔑 Đáp án & Giải thích 12.4
> 1. **(B) since** + mốc thời gian (*2019*).
> 2. **(B) over** — `over the past three years` = cụm chỉ giai đoạn gần đây (tương đương `in/during the past…`), đi với Present Perfect.
> 3. **(B) When** — hỏi mốc thời gian, câu trả lời sẽ ở Past Simple (*last month*).
> 4. **(B) for** + khoảng thời gian (*three months*).
> 5. **(C) since** + mệnh đề (*the committee approved the original plan*).
