## Exercise 11.1: Viết câu hỏi với `How long … ?`

1. (you / be / married?) $\rightarrow$ `How long have you been married?` *(Ví dụ)*
2. (you / know / Mr. Diaz?) $\rightarrow$ [How long have you known Mr. Diaz?]
3. (she / work / for this company?) $\rightarrow$ [How long has she worked for this company?]
4. (you / have / this laptop?) $\rightarrow$ [How long have you had this laptop?]
5. (they / be / on the phone?) $\rightarrow$ [How long have they been on the phone?]
6. (the position / be / vacant?) $\rightarrow$ [How long has the position been vacant?]

> [!success]- 🔑 Đáp án & Giải thích 11.1
> 2. **How long have you known Mr. Diaz?** — `know` là stative verb $\rightarrow$ dạng **đơn**, không dùng ~~have you been knowing~~.
> 3. **How long has she worked for this company?** / **How long has she been working for this company?** — cả hai đều đúng; dạng tiếp diễn nhấn mạnh quá trình. Chủ ngữ `she` số ít $\rightarrow$ `has`.
> 4. **How long have you had this laptop?** — `have` = sở hữu $\rightarrow$ dạng **đơn**.
> 5. **How long have they been on the phone?** — `be` ở đây là trạng thái kéo dài $\rightarrow$ `have been`; `they` số nhiều.
> 6. **How long has the position been vacant?** — câu bị động: `has + been + V3 (vacant)`.

## Exercise 11.2: Hoàn thành câu trả lời với `for` hoặc `since`.

1. I've been working at this company [for] five years.
2. She has lived in Da Nang [since] 2019.
3. We've known each other [for] a long time.
4. The office has been closed [since] last Friday.
5. He has been the sales manager [for] three months.
6. I haven't seen my old colleague [since] we graduated.

> [!success]- 🔑 Đáp án & Giải thích 11.2
> 1. **for** + khoảng thời gian (*five years*).
> 2. **since** + mốc thời gian cụ thể (*2019*).
> 3. **for** (*a long time* = khoảng thời gian).
> 4. **since** + mốc bắt đầu (*last Friday*).
> 5. **for** (*three months* — khoảng thời gian).
> 6. **since** + mệnh đề mốc thời gian (*we graduated*).

## Exercise 11.3: Chọn dạng đúng của động từ.

1. How long (you / be) [have you been] a customer of this bank?
2. How long (Ms. Ito / work) [has Ms. Ito been working] in the accounting department?
3. How long (you / know) [have you known] the answer?
4. How long (the store / be) [has the store been] under renovation?
5. How long (they / wait) [have they been waiting] for the delivery?

> [!success]- 🔑 Đáp án & Giải thích 11.3
> 1. **have you been** — `be` (trạng thái) $\rightarrow$ `have + been`.
> 2. **has Ms. Ito been working** — hành động kéo dài $\rightarrow$ tiếp diễn; chủ ngữ số ít.
> 3. **have you known** — `know` là stative verb $\rightarrow$ **dạng đơn**.
> 4. **has the store been** — bị động/trạng thái kéo dài, `the store` số ít.
> 5. **have they been waiting** — hành động kéo dài tới hiện tại.

## Exercise 11.4: TOEIC Part 5 Mini-drill.

1. \_\_\_\_\_\_ has Mr. Coleman been the head of the marketing team?  
   (A) How often · (B) How long · (C) How many · (D) How much  
   $\rightarrow$ `B` *(Ví dụ: hỏi về khoảng thời gian $\rightarrow$ How long)*
2. The company \_\_\_\_\_\_ this supplier since 2016.  
   (A) uses · (B) used · (C) has been using · (D) is using  
   $\rightarrow$ [C]
3. How long \_\_\_\_\_\_ Ms. Park \_\_\_\_\_\_ in the Seoul branch?  
   (A) has / worked · (B) does / work · (C) is / working · (D) has / working  
   $\rightarrow$ [A]
4. How long \_\_\_\_\_\_ you \_\_\_\_\_\_ the new accounting software?  
   (A) have / been knowing · (B) have / known · (C) do / know · (D) are / knowing  
   $\rightarrow$ [B]
5. The position has been vacant \_\_\_\_\_\_ the previous manager retired.  
   (A) for · (B) since · (C) during · (D) until  
   $\rightarrow$ [B]

> [!success]- 🔑 Đáp án & Giải thích 11.4
> 1. **(B) How long** — hỏi thời lượng $\rightarrow$ `How long has Mr. Coleman been…?`
> 2. **(C) has been using** — `since 2016` + hành động còn tiếp diễn $\rightarrow$ Hiện tại hoàn thành tiếp diễn.
> 3. **(A) has / worked** — `How long has Ms. Park worked…?` (hoặc `has … been working`); loại (B) vì hiện tại đơn không đi với `How long`.
> 4. **(B) have / known** — **bẫy stative verb**: `know` không chia tiếp diễn.
> 5. **(B) since** — `since + mệnh đề` (*the previous manager retired*); `for` chỉ đi với khoảng thời gian.
